01 — About
From Stage to Launchpad

I started my academic journey studying cello performance at Interlochen Arts Academy. As a musician, I learned that excellence requires relentless practice, collaboration, and thoughtful compromise — every ensemble member contributes to a greater whole.

That same mindset now drives my work in aerospace engineering at Rutgers University. Switching from music to engineering meant adapting to completely different ways of thinking, but it taught me how to quickly absorb new concepts, persist through complex challenges, and apply creative thinking to technical problems.

Today I'm building a flapping wing drone under faculty advisement, running airfoil CFD simulations in ANSYS, tutoring SAT students, and serving as treasurer for a campus organization — always learning, always building.
